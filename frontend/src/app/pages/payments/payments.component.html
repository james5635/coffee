Known limitations

  • Staff can only look up an order by ID; there is no shop-wide payment list.
  • The backend has no completed-orders query, so completed orders cannot be discovered here; refunds are limited to orders you can look up.
  • A refund is recorded against one order and cannot exceed the amount collected on it; there is no separate refund-approval workflow.
  • Only pending or accepted orders accept payments, matching backend rules.
